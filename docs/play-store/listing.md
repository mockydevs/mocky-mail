## Full description (4000 characters max)
Mocky Mail is a fast, private email app made by Mocky Digital in Nairobi. It keeps working when your connection doesn't.

ALL YOUR EMAIL IN ONE PLACE
• Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365 and any IMAP mailbox, including company and hosting email.
• Pick your Google account from the phone, or sign in with Microsoft. For other email, type your address and password: the server settings are found for you.
• One inbox for every account, or each account on its own.

MADE FOR SLOW AND PATCHY CONNECTIONS
• Your mail is saved on your phone, so you can read and search it offline.
• Messages you send wait in the Outbox and go out by themselves when you are back online.
• Archive, delete, flag and mark as read work offline too, and catch up later.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• Remote images that tell senders when you opened an email are blocked until you choose to show them.
• Emails are shown in a locked-down viewer.
• Passwords are kept in Android's secure storage. No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your email goes only between your phone and your email provider; Mocky Digital never sees it.

COMFORTABLE TO USE
• A full-screen message view with Reply, Reply all and Forward within thumb reach.
• Quick reply from the bottom of a message, or switch to the full editor.
• Light and dark themes, including dark mode for email bodies.
• New-mail notifications.

Free, with no ads. Made by Mocky Digital, Nairobi, Kenya. Questions: info@mocky.co.ke.
