# Mocky Mail 0.6.0

A search and rules release: find any message with a few words, and let
Mocky Mail sort your mail for you.

One line for the update notice (`latest.json`):

> Smarter search, mail rules and trusted senders.

## Search

- Search understands what you're after. Type words as before, or narrow
  it down: `from:jane`, `to:me@work.com`, `subject:invoice`,
  `has:attachment`, `is:unread`, `category:Clients`, `after:2026-10-01`,
  `before:today`, `in:sent`, `larger:2m`. Put a phrase in quotes, start a
  word with `-` to leave it out, and use `OR` between two terms to find
  either.
- What you've typed shows as chips under the search box; remove one with
  its ×. A mistake, such as a date Mocky Mail can't read, is explained in
  plain words.
- Suggestions as you type: people after `from:` and `to:`, your categories,
  folders and the rest.
- Search tips (the ? by the search box) list everything, with examples you
  can click.
- Choose where to search: this folder, including its subfolders, the
  whole account, or every account. Results from elsewhere show which folder
  they're in.
- Cc is searched too.

## Rules

- Rules sort your mail as it arrives. A rule says which messages it's for
  (from, to, Cc, subject, body, a header, attachments, importance, size,
  a category, or mail sent only to you), which to skip, and what to do:
  move, copy, categorise, flag, mark as read, set importance, pin, delete
  or notify you.
- Make one in Settings › Rules, or from a message with Create rule…. The
  editor shows how many messages in your Inbox it would match as you build
  it.
- Run rules now on any folder, put them in order, switch them on and off,
  and stop later rules from running on a message.
- Keep a rule on the mail server and it runs even when your device is off,
  on servers that take filters (such as Mocky Mail and other mailcow
  servers). Gmail and Outlook keep their own filters, so rules for those
  accounts run on this device.

## Pictures and links

- Always show pictures from a sender, or from their whole company, with
  one click. See and remove trusted senders in Settings.
- Hover a link to see where it really goes. When the link's text shows
  one address but goes to another site, you're warned.

## Also

- The first start after updating rebuilds the search index once, which can
  take a little while on a large mailbox.
