# Mocky Mail 0.4.0

A reliability release: email shows as its sender designed it, nothing you
write or send gets lost, and sync is lighter on your mail server.

One line for the update notice (`latest.json`):

> Email shows as designed, safer sending, lighter sync and a new sign-in screen.

## Reading

- Email keeps its own layout, widths and phone styles, as in Gmail and
  Outlook. Receipts and newsletters are no longer cropped or pushed to one
  side; on a phone, a designed email uses the full width of the screen.
- Pictures attached to a message always show in the attachment list, even
  when the message body doesn't display them.
- Meeting invitations from Outlook show at the right time in every time zone.
- Web pages, SVG pictures, shortcuts and Office files with macros are saved,
  not opened straight from a message.

## Writing and sending

- Leaving a message you are writing (for a folder, Settings or a link) asks
  to save or discard it. Plain Enter in To, Cc, Bcc or Subject no longer sends.
- Undo send and editing a queued message keep the Bcc recipients, and saved
  drafts and Sent keep them too.
- A message that may already have gone out is looked up in Sent before it is
  sent again, and a sent message stays in the Outbox until its copy is in Sent.

## Accounts and sign-in

- A new sign-in screen.
- Adding an account on your own domain finds its mail server even when the
  computer's DNS is slow, and explains a wrong server name in plain words.
- Google and Microsoft accounts can sign in again from Settings when their
  sign-in expires, without removing the account.
- Long Microsoft sign-ins are stored correctly on Windows.
- On Android, Google sign-in says why it failed.

## Sync

- Reading, moving or sending mail no longer logs in again and re-checks every
  folder; the app keeps one connection and checks only what changed.
- Read marks, flags and deletes on a message you just moved reach the server.
- Contacts and calendars sync more reliably: no stuck changes, no lost edits,
  phone and email labels kept.

## Also

- The open conversation stays put when a reply arrives, and Esc while typing
  no longer closes the screen.
- On Android, Back closes an attachment preview before the message.
- If the app can't start, it says why.
- Linux updates are checked again just before they are installed.
