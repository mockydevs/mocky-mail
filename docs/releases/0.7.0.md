# Mocky Mail 0.7.0

A writing release: format your messages the way you want, and send them
the way people expect.

One line for the update notice (`latest.json`):

> A new editor for writing, read receipts and Check Names.

## Writing

- A new editor: fonts, sizes, text colour and highlight, bold, italic,
  underline and strikethrough, bulleted and numbered lists (eight levels
  deep), alignment, links, quotes and tables. What you see is what is sent,
  and every message also carries a plain-text version.
- Paste from a web page or a document and the formatting comes along, minus
  anything unsafe.
- Replies and forwards keep the original message, as it looked, underneath
  your text. Your signature goes above it.
- Find and Replace (Ctrl+F, Ctrl+H) inside the message you're writing.
- Format painter: copy the look of one piece of text onto another
  (double-click it to keep it on).
- Insert a symbol, a contact card (.vcf), or another message as an
  attachment.
- Check Names (Alt+K) turns names you typed into addresses from your
  contacts, and asks when a name fits more than one person. It also runs
  when you press Send.
- On a computer, Pop Out moves the message you're writing into a window of
  its own, and you can write several at once.

## Read receipts

- Ask for a read receipt when you send.
- When a message asks you for one, choose each time, always send, or never
  send (Settings › Reading). Mocky Mail never sends one to an address that
  doesn't match the sender without asking you.
