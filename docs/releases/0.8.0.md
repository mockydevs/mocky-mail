# Mocky Mail 0.8.0

A planning release: a full calendar, tasks, and more out of your
contacts.

One line for the update notice (`latest.json`):

> Day and week calendar views, tasks, reminders and contact groups.

## Calendar

- Day, work week and week views beside month and agenda, with a mini
  month to jump around, a line at the current time, and events that
  overlap shown side by side.
- On a computer, click or drag over empty time and type to add an event;
  drag an event to move it, drag its bottom edge to change its length, and
  hold Ctrl to copy it. The keyboard works too: arrows, Enter, N for new,
  Delete.
- Show or hide each calendar, or put them side by side in the day view.
- Repeat events your way: every few days, on chosen weekdays, monthly by
  date or "the second Tuesday", yearly, ending after a number of times or
  on a date.
- Invite people, and use Find a Time to see when everyone is free. Mocky
  Mail asks before sending invitations, updates and cancellations. Change
  one date of a repeating event or the whole series.
- Show as busy, free, tentative or away, and mark events private.

## Reminders

- One reminders panel for events, tasks and flagged messages: dismiss,
  snooze (from 5 minutes to tomorrow) or open the item.
- Add your own reminders to events in shared or read-only calendars and to
  meetings other people organise; they stay on this device.

## Tasks

- A new Tasks screen: lists, due dates, reminders, importance and
  categories, with Today, Next 7 days, All and Completed views.
- Tasks sync with your mail server's task lists (CalDAV), Google Tasks and
  Microsoft To Do, or stay on this device.
- Turn a message into a task from the message or its flag menu.

## People

- Favourite contacts come first, here and in suggestions as you write.
- Categories and photos on contacts.
- Contact groups: type a group's name in To and everyone in it is added,
  or use Email group.

## Gmail and Microsoft accounts

- Connect a Gmail or Microsoft account's calendar, contacts and tasks with
  one sign-in, and set its automatic replies from Mocky Mail, including a
  separate reply for people outside your organisation on Microsoft 365.
  This needs Google and Microsoft to approve Mocky Mail's access; until
  they do, connecting explains what is missing.
