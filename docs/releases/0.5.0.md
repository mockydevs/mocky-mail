# Mocky Mail 0.5.0

A mail-handling release: work through many messages at once, sort and
organise them your way, and see a message where and how you like.

One line for the update notice (`latest.json`):

> Select many messages at once, categories, pinning, follow-up dates and more ways to sort.

## Many messages at once

- Select several messages: Ctrl-click, Shift-click for a range, the
  checkbox on each row, or Ctrl+A. On a phone, press and hold a message,
  then tap others.
- Then mark them read or unread, flag, pin, categorise, move, copy,
  archive, delete or mark as junk in one go.
- Move to and Copy to open a folder list you can filter by typing
  (shortcut `v`). On a computer you can also drag messages onto a folder;
  hold Ctrl to copy.
- Undo and redo the last 20 changes with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.

## Organising

- Colour categories. Add, rename and recolour them in Settings, and put
  them on messages from the Categorise menu. Gmail labels show as
  categories, and the categories you set become labels in Gmail. On other
  mail servers they are kept as message keywords, where the server allows.
- Pin a message to keep it at the top of its folder.
- Flag a message for today, tomorrow, this week, next week or any date.
  You get a reminder when it is due, and overdue dates show in red.
- Importance: high and low importance show on the message, and you can
  set it when you write.
- Mark as junk and Not junk.
- Favourite folders at the top of the sidebar, and put your accounts in
  the order you want.

## The message list

- Sort by date, sender, subject or size, in either direction, and group
  by date, sender or importance.
- More filters: has attachments, sent to me, and pinned.
- Click a message's left edge to switch it between read and unread.

## Reading

- On a computer, put the reading pane on the right, at the bottom, or
  turn it off.
- Double-click a message to open it in a window of its own.

## Accounts and sync

- Choose how much mail each account keeps on this device: the last 1 to
  24 months, or all of it. Older mail keeps its sender, subject and
  preview, and downloads when you open it.
- Work Offline pauses all syncing and sending until you switch it off.

## Also

- On Android, the app no longer runs under the status bar: the top of
  every screen sits below the clock and signal icons.
- New downloads for Linux (AppImage and `.rpm`) and for Linux and Windows
  on ARM64 computers.
