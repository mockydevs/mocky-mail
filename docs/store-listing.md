## Description
Mocky Mail is a fast, private email app for your computer, made by Mocky Digital in Nairobi.

- Works with any email: Gmail, Outlook, Microsoft 365 and any IMAP mailbox, including company mail.
- Keeps working on bad connections. Your mail is saved on your computer, and messages you send wait in the Outbox and go out by themselves when you are back online.
- Private by default. Remote images that track when you open an email are blocked until you allow them, and email is shown in a locked-down viewer.
- All your accounts in one place, with only the active account expanded so the sidebar stays tidy.
- Light and dark themes, including dark mode for email bodies, with a one-click switch back to original colours.
- New-mail notifications with an optional soft chime.
- Passwords are kept in Windows Credential Manager, never in plain files. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

Free and open source under the GPL-3.0.

## Restricted capability: runFullTrust (justification)
Mocky Mail is a desktop email client built with Tauri (Rust and WebView2). It runs as a full-trust desktop app to connect to the user's own IMAP and SMTP mail servers on their standard ports, to keep a local mail database for offline use, to store account passwords and sign-in tokens in Windows Credential Manager, and to open the system browser for Google and Microsoft sign-in with a loopback redirect. It installs no services or drivers and makes no system-wide changes.
