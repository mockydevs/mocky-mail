Built for how email really works here

Patchy data, many accounts and a lot of marketing mail. Mocky Mail is designed around all three.

Works on bad connections

Your mail is saved on your device, so you can read and search it offline. Messages you send wait in the Outbox and go out by themselves when the connection returns.

Private by default

Images from the internet are blocked until you allow them, which stops tracking pixels from telling senders when you opened their email.

Every account, one inbox

Gmail and Outlook sign in with one click. Mocky Mail, your company domain and any other IMAP email work with a password. See them together or one at a time.

Safe to open

Every email is cleaned before you see it and shown in a locked-down viewer, so scripts and tricks hidden in messages cannot run. Passwords live in your system keychain.

Easy on the eyes

Light and dark themes that follow your computer, and in dark mode your emails go dark too, with one click to see any email in its original colours.

Never miss a message

A notification shows who wrote and what it is about, with a soft chime you can turn off. Fast search covers senders, subjects and message text.

Install on Linux

On Ubuntu, Debian, Mint or Pop!_OS, paste this into a terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mockydevs/mocky-mail/main/install.sh | bash

It downloads the latest version, checks its checksum and installs it with apt, asking for your password only for apt. Run it again any time to update. Read the script first if you like.

Or install the downloaded file yourself: sudo apt install ./mocky-mail_0.2.1_amd64.deb. To remove it: sudo apt remove mocky-mail (your mail and settings stay).

Install on Windows

  1. Download mocky-mail_0.2.1_x64-setup.exe and open it.
  2. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may say it protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Follow the steps, then open Mocky Mail from the Start menu and add your email account.

To update, run the newer installer. To remove it, use Settings, Apps, Installed apps.

Check your download (SHA-256)

To confirm a file is genuine, compare its checksum. On Linux run sha256sum mocky-mail_0.2.1_amd64.deb; on Windows run certutil -hashfile mocky-mail_0.2.1_x64-setup.exe SHA256 in Command Prompt. Also listed in SHA256SUMS.

Questions

Is Mocky Mail free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use, with no ads. It is open source, so anyone can check how it handles their mail.

Which email accounts work?

Gmail and Outlook, Hotmail and Microsoft 365 sign in with their own login page. Mocky Mail addresses, company email on your own domain, Yahoo, iCloud and any other provider that offers IMAP work with your password or an app password.

Where is my mail stored?

On your computer or phone, and it stays on your email server as before. Passwords and sign-in tokens are kept in your system keychain, never in plain files.

Do I still have webmail?

Yes. If your email is hosted with Mocky Digital you can still sign in at mail.mailer.co.ke from any browser.

When is macOS coming?

Soon. It will appear on this page as soon as it is ready, with the same features.
